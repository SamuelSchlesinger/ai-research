# Research snapshot, 9 November 2026

This snapshot preserves completed material and explicitly unfinished research. It is not a claim that all assertions in the source reports have passed review.

- `workspace/threshold_paper/explainer.md`: corrected explanation of the exact $5n^2/16$ upper bound and the sign-coherent lower bound. The independent review and its checks are in `experiments/explainer_review/`; that review describes the earlier draft, and its findings motivated corrections.
- `experiments/coherence_fable/`: completed exploratory report on coherence, random arrangements, drift, near-zero-sum structure, and one-table-bit gates, with scripts and numerical logs. Its PROVED labels are the author's claims; independent review remains ongoing.
- `experiments/review_coherence/`: **in-progress Astra referee snapshot**, not a final acceptance. Its first drift audit requires comparability to the maximum address weight across ALL blocks, including a lower bound on every weight in the chosen block. Local within-block comparability alone is insufficient. Do not generalize the near-zero-sum conclusion before the structural theorem audit is complete.
- `experiments/one_table_bit/`: **in-progress Astra research snapshot** on arbitrary-address-weight gates reading at most one table bit. The dominance condition needs the correct intercept; no improved exponent is certified by this snapshot summary.
- `experiments/extractors_astra/`: **in-progress Astra research snapshot** on modern extractors for exact and average-case upper and lower bounds. Ordinary Andreev must be distinguished from extractor-based variants. This is not a completed literature review or a new extractor theorem.

The current paper already gives the improved average-case upper bounds $O(n^{3/2}\sqrt{\log(1/\delta)})$ for error $\delta$, and $O(\varepsilon^2n^{3/2}\sqrt{\log(1/\varepsilon)})$ in its stated advantage range. These are inherited results, not discoveries of the new extractor investigation. Section 17 of RESEARCH_STATE.md supersedes the older average-case baseline in section 2.

The three Astra investigations were running during capture. Files are individually captured snapshots, not an atomic checkpoint of a completed run; later output is not included.
